What the range tells you

Residuals are kept together by issue date, preserving movement across vegetables. Marginal interval endpoints are never added to form a basket interval.

Overlapping weeks, a small calibration sample and changing market conditions limit the evidence. An 80% label is a target, not a guarantee.

What this study has established

A reproducible historical comparison and a working budgeting workflow. Original publication timestamps and historical weather vintages are unavailable.

No buyer pilot, verified savings, invoice-cost accuracy or national impact has been established. A prospective pilot and a fuller source audit remain next steps.

Project guide

The forecasting contract

Each target is the average CBSL Dambulla wholesale point price on valid observation days in the seven calendar days after issue, excluding issue day. At least four quotes per vegetable and all three labels are required for an evaluated basket.

The central basket estimate adds quantity × each vegetable’s central price. This sum is not necessarily the median basket cost. The range uses 105 aligned calibration scenarios, not a sum of individual price ranges.

Regional weather signals

NASA POWER daily API ↗ supplies rainfall, temperature and humidity for three regional grid proxies: Nuwara Eliya, Badulla and Kandy/Matale.

Weather features end at least seven days before issue. Grid proxies aren’t farm stations, district averages or verified supply weights. The source’s Local Solar Time differs from Sri Lanka’s civil-day boundary.
